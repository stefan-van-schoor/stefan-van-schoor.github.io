Stefan van Schoor

Connect on LinkedIn

With a Bachelor of Science (Honors) in Computer Science from the University of Pretoria, I have developed a strong technical foundation and a keen interest in cybersecurity. I currently work as an Intermediate Security Consultant at Whirly Labs (Pty) Ltd, where I conduct security assessments for predominantly European clients, helping identify and address vulnerabilities to strengthen the security of their systems and applications. I have a particular interest in organisations that integrate AI models into their products and services, exploring the unique security challenges that come with emerging technologies.

Previously, I worked as a Cyber Security Consultant at MWR CyberSec, conducting penetration tests for major financial institutions and organisations within South Africa's financial sector. My experience includes identifying and validating security vulnerabilities, producing detailed reports with actionable recommendations and developing automation scripts to improve testing efficiency.

While my professional experience is primarily rooted in offensive security, I also have a strong interest in defensive security, particularly information security and the broader practices involved in protecting an organisation's information assets. I am passionate about understanding security from both offensive and defensive perspectives and applying that knowledge to develop practical, effective solutions that strengthen an organisation's overall security posture.
